### 4\. Ratio and Proportion

Q7. The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 3:2. If there are 30 boys, how many girls are there?

A) 15 B) 20 C) 25 D) 40

Answer: B) 20

Explanation:

3 parts = 30 boys

1 part = 30 ÷ 3 = 10

Girls = 2 parts = 2 × 10 = 20

Q8. Divide ₹600 in the ratio 2:3.

A) ₹200, ₹400 B) ₹240, ₹360 C) ₹300, ₹300 D) ₹250, ₹350

Answer: B) ₹240, ₹360

Explanation:

Total parts = 2 + 3 = 5

One part = 600 ÷ 5 = 120

Shares = 2 × 120 = ₹240 and 3 × 120 = ₹360

### 9\. Number System

Q17. What is the LCM of 12 and 18?

A) 24 B) 30 C) 36 D) 72

Answer: C) 36

Explanation:

Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36, 48...

Multiples of 18: 18, 36, 54...

The smallest common multiple is 36.

Q18. What is the HCF of 24 and 36?

A) 6 B) 8 C) 12 D) 18

Answer: C) 12

Explanation:

Factors common to both include 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 12.

The highest common factor is 12.

### 11\. Compound Interest

Q21. Find the compound interest on ₹1,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years, compounded annually.

A) ₹100 B) ₹200 C) ₹210 D) ₹220

Answer: C) ₹210

Explanation:

After Year 1: ₹1,000 + ₹100 = ₹1,100

After Year 2: ₹1,100 + ₹110 = ₹1,210

Compound interest = 1,210 − 1,000 = ₹210
